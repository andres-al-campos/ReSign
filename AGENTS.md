# ReSign

Keep your sideloaded iOS apps from expiring, without having to remember them.

Features are mapped in `features/`; read the map before adding one.

## Principles

- **A problem that blocks every project gets a banner that stays until it's
  fixed.** A notification can be closed by accident and a per-project error
  repeats the same news on every row. The banner sits at the top of the
  popup, has no close button, and turns the menu bar icon red. (2026-10-21,
  after an iOS 27 update left Xcode 26 unable to install.)
